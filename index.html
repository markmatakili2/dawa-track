How it works

1. Register

Create an account with your facility and licensing details.

2. Get verified

An administrator reviews your facility before requests and listings unlock.

3. Browse and request

Verified facilities can find suitable stock and submit transfer requests.

4. Coordinate transfer

Administrators broker requests between the facilities involved.

Only licensed, verified facilities can see medicine listings and request or list stock.
